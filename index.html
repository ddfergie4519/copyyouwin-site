Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is AEO, and how is it different from regular SEO?

Traditional SEO gets your website ranked in Google's list of links. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) makes sure AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity can actually understand your business well enough to recommend it directly — since more people are asking AI for recommendations instead of scrolling search results.

Do you write website copy too, or just optimize for AI visibility?

Both. AEO only works if the underlying content is clear and specific in the first place — so most projects start with rewriting website copy, then layering in the technical pieces (schema markup, directory listings, structured FAQs) that help AI tools pick it up.

Which AI platforms do you check?

ChatGPT, Google (including AI Overviews), Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot are the core five. We run real searches on each to see whether — and how — your business actually shows up.

What size or type of business do you typically work with?

We work primarily with small, owner-operated businesses — think bookkeepers, contractors, restaurants, law firms, insurance agents, and consultants. If you're the kind of business people find through word-of-mouth and local reputation, but haven't quite caught up in how AI tools represent you online, that's exactly who we help.

Do you work with businesses outside the Kansas City area?

We're based in Kansas City, but the work itself — website copy, technical fixes, AI-visibility optimization — doesn't require us to be in the same room. We're happy to work with businesses anywhere; a video call covers everything an in-person meeting would.

I already have a website — do I still need this?

Most likely, yes. Having a website isn't the same as being visible to AI tools — plenty of well-built sites are still invisible when someone asks ChatGPT or Gemini for a recommendation. A free consultation will show you exactly where you stand.

How much does it cost to get started?

Projects typically start around $750–$1,200 for a focused Quick Fix, scaling up depending on scope. Every project starts with a free consultation, so you'll know the investment before committing to anything.

How long does the process typically take?

Most engagements run a few weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how much of the site needs rework. Directory and listing work can trail a bit longer since some platforms require manual verification outside our control.

What happens in the free consultation?

We'll look at how your business currently shows up — or doesn't — across AI tools and search, and walk through exactly what's working, what's missing, and what a fix would involve. No pressure, no obligation.
